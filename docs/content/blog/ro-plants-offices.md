Safe drinking water in an office, school or factory is a daily necessity, and the purifier is used by dozens or hundreds of people. Choosing one that suits your water source and your headcount — and keeping it maintained — matters more than the brand on the front.

## Test your water first

Different water sources need different treatment. Before choosing:

- Find out whether your supply is municipal, borewell or tanker water.
- Get the water tested for total dissolved solids (TDS) and any other contaminants of concern.
- Share the report with suppliers; a good supplier will recommend the purification method from it.

RO (reverse osmosis) is widely used for high-TDS water, but water with low TDS may not need RO at all.

## Capacity: how much water per day

- Estimate daily drinking water need from your headcount, with extra for peak days.
- Commercial RO plants are rated in litres per hour; ask about storage tank size so water is available at peak times like lunch.
- For schools, plan for breaks when many students drink at once.

## Dispensing

- Separate purifier and cooler, or combined units.
- Number of taps and their placement to avoid crowding.
- Hot water option for pantries if needed.

## Waste water

RO systems reject part of the water as waste. Ask:

1. What proportion of water is rejected by the system.
2. Whether the reject water can be reused for cleaning, gardening or flushing.

## Maintenance

- Filters and membranes need periodic replacement.
- Ask about an **annual maintenance contract**, what it covers, and response time for breakdowns.
- Keep a service log near the unit.

## Installation

Check space, plumbing, drainage and electrical points before installation. Ask whether the supplier handles plumbing and electrical connections.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Choosing RO without testing water.** If your water already has low dissolved solids, RO may be unnecessary and wastes water.
- **Undersizing for peak times.** Lunch hours and school breaks drain small storage tanks quickly; size for the rush, not the average.
- **Skipping filter changes.** A neglected purifier can make water worse, not better. Keep to the service schedule.
- **Letting reject water go down the drain.** Plan a simple way to reuse it for cleaning or gardening.
- **Placing the unit in direct sun or a damp corner.** Heat and moisture shorten component life; choose a shaded, dry spot with good access for servicing.

## What to include in your enquiry

- Type of premises and headcount
- Water source and test report if available
- Required capacity and number of dispensing points
- Cooling or hot water needs
- Installation, AMC and your location
