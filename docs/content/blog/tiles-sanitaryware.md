In a commercial space, floors and washrooms take far more wear than at home — more footfall, more cleaning, less patience for repairs. Buying tiles and sanitaryware that suit that use, and in consistent batches, saves both money and headaches.

## Choose tiles for where they go

- **Vitrified tiles** are dense and hard-wearing — a common choice for office and shop floors.
- **Ceramic tiles** are often used on walls, where wear is lower.
- **Anti-skid or matte finishes** suit washrooms, entrances, ramps and kitchens; glossy tiles can be slippery when wet.
- **Outdoor areas** need tiles rated for outdoor use and weather.

Ask the supplier which tiles are suited to heavy commercial footfall.

## Size, thickness and finish

- Larger tiles mean fewer joints and a cleaner look, but need a level base and skilled laying.
- Check thickness for floors, especially where trolleys or heavy furniture move.
- Look at the tile in daylight and under your lighting before finalising a colour.

## Batch, shade and wastage

Tiles from different production batches can differ slightly in shade.

1. Buy the full quantity from **one batch** where possible, and check batch numbers on the cartons.
2. Add a margin for cutting and breakage — ask your contractor how much for your layout.
3. Keep a few spare boxes for future repairs; matching later can be hard.

## Sanitaryware and fittings

For washrooms in offices, clinics and shops:

- Wall-hung or floor-mounted closets, wash basins and urinals — decide based on space and cleaning ease.
- Ask about flush systems and water consumption.
- For taps and fittings, check the finish and whether spares are easily available.
- Consider accessible fittings where the public or elderly visitors use the washroom.

## Brands, warranty and delivery

- Ask for warranty details on sanitaryware and fittings.
- Check how tiles are packed and whether breakage in transit is replaced.
- Confirm delivery to site and unloading, since tiles are heavy.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Buying tiles in instalments from different batches.** Shade differences show up clearly on large floors. Buy the full quantity at once.
- **Glossy tiles in wet areas.** They look good in a showroom and become slippery in a washroom.
- **Forgetting skirting and steps.** Order matching skirting, step and nosing tiles along with floor tiles.
- **No spares.** Keep a few boxes aside for future repairs.
- **Choosing fittings without spares support.** Taps and flush parts wear out; brands with easily available spares save you money over the years.

## What to include in your enquiry

- Area in square feet for each space (floor, wall, washroom, outdoor)
- Tile type, size, finish and colour preference
- Sanitaryware items and quantities
- Brand preference, if any
- Site location, delivery date and whether you need GST invoices
